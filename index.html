YOUR SCHOOL. YOUR JOURNEY.

Supporting every stage of the school experience.

From everyday communication to serious health concerns, CIRCA gives schools one organized place to receive reports, review cases, assign the appropriate staff, record actions and follow up.

A student can raise a concern, a teacher can report something they notice, and authorized staff can manage the case according to the school's procedures. School administrators can also manage roles, permissions and school-wide information.

WHY CIRCA?

Built around the way schools actually work.

CIRCA is designed as a multi-school platform. Every school has its own environment, people, roles, permissions and records, while users are automatically connected to the school assigned to their account.

🏫

Multi-School System

One CIRCA platform can support many schools while keeping each school's environment separate.

🔐

Role-Based Access

School owners can create roles and decide what teachers, health officers and support staff can access.

👤

Automatic School Access

Users do not choose their school at login. CIRCA already knows which school belongs to their account.

🛡️

Privacy & Security

Sensitive student information can be restricted to authorized people through permissions.

⚙️

Flexible Roles

Schools can add or remove roles such as Animateur, Animatrice, Health Officer or Discipline Director.

📈

Reports & Insights

Authorized administrators can review school-wide information and identify areas that need attention.
