DESIGNED WITH CARE

Access should follow responsibility.

CIRCA is designed around school-specific workspaces and role-based permissions. A teacher, health officer, discipline officer and school owner should not automatically see the same information.

  • School-specific staff invitations
  • Approval before protected access
  • Separate health and discipline permissions
  • Audit trails for important actions
