Security and audit rules

Production requirements for CIRCA Academy.

  • Only the verified platform administrator can create school invitations.
  • School links allow applications only; they do not activate schools automatically.
  • Each invitation should use a random, single-use, expiring token stored securely on the server.
  • Verify the school and authorized representative before activation.
  • Log approvals, rejections, invitation revocations and administrator changes.
  • Do not grant platform admins unrestricted access to confidential student health or discipline records by default.
